## What is an AI Agent?
An AI agent is a system where an LLM autonomously decides what actions to take to accomplish a goal. Unlike a simple chatbot that just responds, an agent can:
* Plan multi-step tasks
* Use tools (calculators, APIs, databases)
* Observe results and adapt its approach
* Work autonomously until the task is complete

## Key Points
- Can reason about what to do next
- Calls tools with specific parameters
- Observes tool results
- Repeats until task is done
- Can be simple, multi-agent, or autonomous
