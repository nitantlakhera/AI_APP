## Generative AI

Generative AI refers to artificial intelligence systems that can create new content, such as text, images, music, code, and more. Unlike traditional AI that classifies or predicts, generative models produce content based on patterns learned from vast amounts of existing data.

## Key Technologies

### Large Language Models (LLMs)

* GPT-4, GPT-4o: Trained on billions of text examples, these models are the foundation of generative AI.
* Llama 3: Developed by Meta, this model is a significant improvement over previous versions.
* Claude: Provided by Anthropic, this model uses a unique approach to generate text.
* DALL-E, Stable Diffusion: These models generate images from text, offering a range of creative possibilities.
* Gemini: Developed by Google, this model is designed for high-quality text generation.

### Diffusion Models

* DALL-E: Uses a diffusion model to generate images from text.
* Stable Diffusion: Offers a range of image generation options, from simple to complex.

### Transformers

* The neural network architecture behind most modern AI, including generative models.
* Used in various applications, including text generation, image synthesis, and more.

## Popular LLM Providers

* OpenAI (GPT-4, GPT-4o)
* Meta (Llama 3)
* Anthropic (Claude)
* Google (Gemini)
* Local models via Ollama (free, runs on your computer)

## Applications

* Chatbots and virtual assistants
* Code generation (GitHub Copilot, Cursor)
* Content creation and summarization
* Translation and language learning
* Research and analysis
