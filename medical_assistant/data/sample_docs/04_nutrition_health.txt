Nutrition and Health — Educational Guide
========================================

DISCLAIMER: General education only. Consult a dietitian for personal nutrition plans.

Balanced Diet
-------------
A balanced diet includes:
- Fruits and vegetables (vitamins, minerals, fiber)
- Whole grains (energy, fiber)
- Protein (meat, fish, eggs, legumes — muscle repair)
- Healthy fats (nuts, olive oil, avocado)
- Water (hydration — essential for all body functions)

Macronutrients
--------------
- Carbohydrates: primary energy source (grains, fruits, vegetables)
- Proteins: building blocks for muscles, enzymes, hormones
- Fats: energy storage, cell membranes, hormone production

Micronutrients
--------------
- Vitamins: A, B complex, C, D, E, K — various body functions
- Minerals: iron, calcium, zinc, potassium — bones, blood, nerves

BMI (Body Mass Index) — Educational
------------------------------------
BMI = weight (kg) / height (m)²
- Under 18.5: underweight
- 18.5-24.9: normal range
- 25-29.9: overweight
- 30+: obese

BMI is a screening tool, not a diagnosis. Athletes may have high BMI due to muscle.
Healthcare providers use BMI alongside other measures.

Hydration
---------
- Adults typically need 2-3 liters of fluid per day (varies by activity, climate)
- Water is the best choice for hydration
- Dehydration signs: thirst, dark urine, dizziness, fatigue

Healthy Habits
--------------
- Regular physical activity (150 min moderate exercise per week — WHO guideline)
- Adequate sleep (7-9 hours for adults)
- Stress management
- Avoid smoking and excessive alcohol
- Regular health check-ups
