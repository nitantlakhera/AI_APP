Common Health Conditions — Educational Overview
===============================================

DISCLAIMER: Educational content only. Not for self-diagnosis. See a doctor.

Common Cold
-----------
- Caused by viruses (rhinoviruses most common)
- Symptoms: runny nose, sore throat, cough, mild fever
- Treatment: rest, fluids, symptom relief — usually resolves in 7-10 days
- Prevention: hand washing, avoiding close contact with sick people

Hypertension (High Blood Pressure)
----------------------------------
- Blood pressure consistently above normal (130/80 mmHg or higher)
- Often called "silent killer" — may have no symptoms
- Risk factors: diet, obesity, stress, genetics, age
- Management: lifestyle changes, medication under doctor supervision

Diabetes (Type 2) — Educational
---------------------------------
- Body does not use insulin effectively (insulin resistance)
- Symptoms: increased thirst, frequent urination, fatigue, blurred vision
- Management: diet, exercise, medication, blood sugar monitoring
- Early detection through screening is important

Asthma — Educational
--------------------
- Chronic condition affecting airways in the lungs
- Symptoms: wheezing, shortness of breath, chest tightness, coughing
- Triggers: allergens, exercise, cold air, stress
- Managed with inhalers and avoiding triggers under medical guidance

When to Seek Medical Care
-------------------------
- Chest pain or difficulty breathing
- Sudden severe headache
- High fever that does not respond to medication
- Signs of allergic reaction (swelling, difficulty breathing)
- Any symptom that is severe, sudden, or worrying

Call emergency services (911 / 112 / 999) for life-threatening emergencies.
