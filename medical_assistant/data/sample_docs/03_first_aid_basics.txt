First Aid Basics — Educational Guide
====================================

DISCLAIMER: Educational only. Take a certified first aid course for real emergencies.

Primary Survey (DRSABC)
-----------------------
D — Danger: check scene is safe for you and the patient
R — Response: check if person is conscious (talk, tap shoulder)
S — Send for help: call emergency services if needed
A — Airway: ensure airway is open
B — Breathing: check for normal breathing
C — CPR: start chest compressions if not breathing (if trained)

CPR Basics (Educational)
------------------------
- 30 chest compressions followed by 2 rescue breaths (if trained)
- Compress at least 5 cm deep, rate 100-120 per minute
- Continue until help arrives or person recovers
- AED (defibrillator) can be used if available — follow device instructions

Bleeding Control
----------------
- Apply direct pressure with clean cloth
- Elevate injured limb if possible
- Do not remove embedded objects — stabilize and seek help
- Call emergency services for severe bleeding

Burns
-----
- Cool the burn under running cool water for 20 minutes
- Do not use ice, butter, or ointments on serious burns
- Cover with clean non-stick dressing
- Seek medical help for large, deep, or facial burns

Choking (Adult)
----------------
- Encourage coughing if person can cough effectively
- Back blows: 5 sharp blows between shoulder blades
- Abdominal thrusts (Heimlich): if back blows fail
- Call emergency services if obstruction is not cleared

Always recommend certified first aid training for hands-on skills.
