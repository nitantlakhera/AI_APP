Human Body Basics — Educational Overview
=======================================

DISCLAIMER: This content is for learning only, not medical advice.

The Human Body Systems
----------------------
The human body has several major systems that work together:

1. Circulatory System — heart and blood vessels transport oxygen and nutrients
2. Respiratory System — lungs bring in oxygen and remove carbon dioxide
3. Digestive System — breaks down food and absorbs nutrients
4. Nervous System — brain, spinal cord, and nerves control body functions
5. Musculoskeletal System — bones and muscles provide structure and movement
6. Immune System — defends against infections and disease

Vital Signs (Educational)
-------------------------
- Temperature: normal adult body temperature is approximately 37°C (98.6°F)
- Heart rate: resting adult heart rate is typically 60-100 beats per minute
- Blood pressure: normal is around 120/80 mmHg (varies by individual)
- Respiratory rate: 12-20 breaths per minute for adults at rest

Students should learn that vital signs are measured by healthcare professionals
and interpreted in context of the whole patient, not in isolation.

Common Medical Roles
--------------------
- General Practitioner (GP): primary care, first point of contact
- Nurse: patient care, monitoring, education
- Specialist: focused expertise (cardiology, dermatology, etc.)
- Pharmacist: medication expertise and safety
